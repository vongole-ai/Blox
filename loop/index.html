How to move around

Scroll wheel
Zoom in and out where the cursor is
Drag
Move the canvas in any direction
Click
Go to a photo. Click again to go back
Arrows
Move and change the drift direction
Space
Pause or resume the drift
+ / −
Zoom from the center
0
